AgniLens: Unified Satellite Fire Intelligence

One Earth. Two Satellite Systems. One Unified Fire Intelligence Platform. AgniLens brings MODIS and VIIRS active-fire detections into one place, matches them as candidate correspondences in space and time, and shows historical activity without mistaking detection counts for individual fires.
